Privacy Policy

Local Data Only

All personal data, settings, liked songs, playlists, recently played tracks, and queue information are stored locally in your browser using LocalStorage. We do not collect, store, or transmit this information to any external server.

Third-Party APIs

When you use the music player, search queries and playback requests are sent directly from your browser to the third-party music services you configure or that are enabled by default (Audius, Jamendo, YouTube, Spotify). Those services may log your requests according to their own privacy policies. We do not act as an intermediary or collect that data.

API Keys

Any API keys or credentials you enter in the settings panel are saved only in your browser's LocalStorage. They are not sent to our servers or shared with anyone. You are responsible for keeping your credentials secure.

Cookies & Tracking

This application does not use tracking cookies or analytics. The only cookies or cached data are those required by the service worker to allow the app to work offline as a Progressive Web App (PWA).

Data Control

You can clear all saved data at any time by using the "Reset All Settings" button in the player settings or by clearing your browser's LocalStorage for this origin.
